JIJU'S COLORFUL BIRTHDAY BASH 🎉

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file first.
2. Open the extracted folder in VS Code (File > Open Folder).
3. Open index.html and click Go Live (if Live Server is installed), or double-click index.html to open it in your browser.

ADD PHOTOS
1. Open the website in your browser.
2. Scroll to the photo gallery and click ADD YOUR PHOTOS.
3. Select multiple photos from your laptop. They will appear in the gallery while the page is open.
4. For photos to travel with the website when you share it, put copies in an images folder and add them into the HTML or ask for help deploying it.

MUSIC
Click the music button at the top and choose an audio file from your device.

PERSONALIZE
Click EDIT NAME & MESSAGE to enter your Jiju's preferred name and a custom birthday message.

FILES
index.html = page content
style.css = colorful design and animations
script.js = interactions, photo gallery, music and confetti

Note: photos and music selected through the browser are local to that browser session and are not automatically saved into the project.
